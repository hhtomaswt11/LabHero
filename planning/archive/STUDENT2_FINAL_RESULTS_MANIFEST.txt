STUDENT.2 — Final Results

Adds a campaign-completion summary that opens automatically once per save:
- Normal: after Mission 40, denominator 40 missions / 200 points.
- Easy: after Mission 36, denominator 11 missions / 55 points.

Displayed:
- Student
- Mode
- Score
- Missions completed
- Hint levels unlocked
- Missions using hints

The summary filters score/hints to the active campaign route. Skipped Easy
missions never count. Historic legacy-unscored missions are reported explicitly
rather than receiving fabricated scores.

Persistence:
- Adds final_results_seen inside player_state only.
- Existing six-field save payload is unchanged.
- Old saves default to unseen and may receive the summary once if already complete.

No mission scientific criteria, simulator logic, map data, backend or deployment
files are changed.

Easy Mission 36 completion dialogue now directs the student to close the dialogue to view results.
