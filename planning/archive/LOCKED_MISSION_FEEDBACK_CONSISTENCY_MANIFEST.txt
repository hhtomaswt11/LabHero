Locked mission feedback consistency

Normal M36-M40:

  the scientist dialogue panel.

  Complete Mission XX first!
- The NPC interaction closes immediately after queuing the message.

Easy campaign:

  predecessor resolved from CampaignContext.

Teacher mode:

  are not used.

No mission validator, evidence, objective, threshold, save schema, map, simulator,
backend or deployment logic was changed.
