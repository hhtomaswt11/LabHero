CONTENT.1 — Scientific labels and units

Canonical player-visible terminology
------------------------------------
Predicted growth rate
  Unit: h^-1
  Meaning: flux through the model biomass reaction, used as the model's
  predicted specific growth-rate proxy under the selected assumptions.

Reaction / exchange flux
  Unit: mmol gDW^-1 h^-1

Configured reaction / exchange bound
  Unit: mmol gDW^-1 h^-1

Total absolute flux
  Unit: mmol gDW^-1 h^-1
  Meaning: sum of absolute flux magnitudes in the returned flux vector.

lMOMA total absolute flux adjustment
  Unit: mmol gDW^-1 h^-1
  Meaning: flux-adjustment magnitude relative to the explicit reference.

No flux unit is attached to:
- solver status
- active-reaction counts
- ROOM significant-change score/count
- percentages
- fold changes
- GPR-disabled-reaction counts
- ROOM delta / epsilon parameters

Objective-display rule
----------------------
If the selected objective is the biomass reaction:
  display the objective result as Predicted growth rate (h^-1).

If the selected objective is not the biomass reaction:
  keep Objective flux / Primary objective flux in mmol gDW^-1 h^-1 and show
  Predicted growth rate separately when biomass-reaction flux is available.

Scope
