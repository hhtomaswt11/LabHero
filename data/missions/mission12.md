# Mission 12 — Competing Byproducts

## Theme
Flux diagnostics and byproduct evidence.

## Learning goal
Understand that optimizing a target product is not enough by itself. A strain can produce the desired metabolite while also secreting other byproducts that compete for carbon and flux.

## Scenario
Dr. Almeida wants the player to move from a broad secretion fingerprint to a more focused comparison between one target product and possible competing byproducts.

## Target product
Succinate.

## Core idea
The player should use the objective, environment and Production Flux panel to support a conclusion with evidence. The mission should not be solved through gene knockouts.

## Briefing
In metabolic engineering, a useful analysis checks both the target product and other products secreted in the same condition. These byproducts can reveal whether the flux distribution is really aligned with the desired design.

The player must compare the target product with possible competing byproducts and use New Results to decide when the analysis is ready.
