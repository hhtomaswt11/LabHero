# Mission 10 — Multi-Knockout Robust Design

## Theme
Advanced strain design: objective choice, environmental constraints, production-flux evidence, and a two-gene knockout design.

## Learning goal
The player learns that robust strain design requires more than a high production value. A good solution must combine production, viability and evidence from relevant fluxes.

## Scenario
Dr. Nova gives the player the final challenge of the Advanced Strain Design Lab: build a robust *E. coli* design for lactate production. The exact objective, environmental reaction, tracked fluxes and knockout pair are not provided.

## Mission setup
Target product: lactate.

The design must combine:
- an objective that supports the target product;
- a biologically coherent environmental constraint;
- exactly two knockouts from the candidate list;
- production-flux evidence;
- viable growth.

## Candidate genes
- `b0903`
- `b2297`
- `b0723`
- `b3115`
- `b0728`
- `b1241`

## Briefing idea
Two knockouts can interact in unexpected ways. Blocking one route may redirect flux into another pathway, but blocking too much can damage growth. The challenge is to test candidate pairs, compare the feedback from New Results, and find a design that improves lactate production without making the strain too weak.

Use the Production Flux evidence to support the design instead of relying only on the objective value.
