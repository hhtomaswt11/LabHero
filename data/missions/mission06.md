# Mission 06 — Growth vs Production Challenge

## Theme
Metabolic engineering: balancing growth and product formation.

## Learning goal
The player learns that a good strain design is not defined by production alone. A useful design must keep *E. coli* viable while redirecting part of its metabolism toward a target product.

## Scenario
Dr. Carter presents a competitive challenge: a rival scientist claims to have a better ethanol-producing *E. coli* strain.

The player must test different designs and beat the rival score by finding a strong balance between growth and ethanol production.

## Mission setup
Target product: ethanol.

The challenge score combines:
- growth;
- ethanol production.

A higher score means the design performs better overall, but increasing one part of the score may reduce the other.

## Briefing idea
Growth and production often compete for the same metabolic resources. A design that produces more is not always better if the organism becomes too weak.

Use the simulation results to compare different strategies. Pay attention to both growth and product formation, and remember that adding more changes does not always improve the final result.
