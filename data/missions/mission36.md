# Mission 36 — Oxygen-Capped Fermentation Onset

Vale has opened the first yeast systems challenge.

Build a controlled wild-type pFBA reference in the yeast simulator, then keep the same objective, genotype and base medium while sweeping only the lower bound of glucose uptake. Compare realised oxygen uptake with ethanol secretion across the tested rows.

Your task is to identify the **first tested glucose lower bound** where the fixed oxygen uptake capacity is binding and ethanol secretion is positive.

Do not infer the answer from a configured bound alone: use the measured flux evidence.
