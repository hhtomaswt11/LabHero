# Mission 04 — Knockout for Production

## NPC
Dr. Silva

## Theme
Genes and knockouts: using a knockout to redirect metabolism toward production.

## Objective
Identify which gene knockout can increase succinate production under default/aerobic conditions.

## Target product
- Product: succinate
- Production reaction: `EX_succ_e`

## Correct answer
- Gene id: `b0723`
- Common name: `sdhA`

## Candidate genes
`b0723`, `b2297`, `b1241`, `b3115`, `b0728`, `b2975`

## Player tasks
1. Activate Mission 04 with Dr. Silva after completing Mission 03.
2. Go to the simulation computer.
3. Keep environmental conditions unchanged.
4. Test one candidate gene knockout at a time.
5. Check the Mission 04 Production Check shown in New Results.
6. Return to Dr. Silva and report the production gene.

## Pedagogical idea
Mission 03 teaches that some knockouts can stop growth by removing an essential gene. Mission 04 adds a new idea: some knockouts can redirect metabolism toward a useful product without being framed only as survival/death.
