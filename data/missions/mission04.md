# Mission 04 — Knockout for Production

## NPC
Dr. Silva

## Theme
Genes and knockouts: using genetic changes to redirect metabolism toward production.

## Challenge
Investigate whether disabling one candidate gene can increase ethanol production while keeping the model viable.

## Target product
- Product: ethanol

## Candidate genes
`b0728`, `b1241`, `b2975`, `b2297`, `b0723`

## What to compare
Use the same default environment for every test and compare how each candidate knockout affects production and growth.

## Pedagogical idea
Mission 03 shows that some genes are essential for survival. Mission 04 introduces another use of knockouts: redirecting metabolic flux toward a product of interest.
