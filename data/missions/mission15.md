# Mission 15 — Final Diagnostic Report

## Scientist
Dr. Almeida

## Theme
Flux diagnostics and pathway evidence.

## Learning goal
Show that a metabolic-engineering result should be justified with a full diagnostic report, not only with a single objective value.

## Scenario
The player must combine method choice, objective choice, environmental reasoning, knockout design and production-flux evidence to support a final succinate-production design.

## Main concepts
- pFBA as a parsimonious simulation method
- target product flux
- competing byproducts
- environmental constraints
- gene knockout effect
- evidence-based model interpretation

## Briefing idea
A useful design needs evidence. The player should track the full product panel and show that the target product dominates the byproduct profile while the unwanted route remains controlled.
