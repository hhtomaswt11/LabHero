EASY.1A — Campaign mode architecture

Purpose
- Make campaign mode a first-class, persisted piece of campaign state.
- Preserve the current Normal campaign exactly.
- Prepare Easy/Teacher metadata without exposing an unfinished Easy mode.

Runtime changes
- code/campaign.py: central Normal/Easy/Teacher sequences and helper APIs.
- code/settings.py: campaign_mode defaults to "normal" in player_state.
- code/player.py: restores and serializes campaign_mode inside player_state.
- code/level.py: progression gates use the restored campaign context.

Important
- Six-field save schema is unchanged.
- Historic saves without campaign_mode migrate to Normal.
- Easy mission selection is intentionally NOT configured in EASY.1A.
- No mission validator, scientific logic, score/hint logic, TMX, or deploy file changed.
- EASY.2 will choose the pedagogical Easy mission sequence.
