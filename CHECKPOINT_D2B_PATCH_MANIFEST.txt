LabHero Checkpoint D.2B - Opaque Ground Surface

Purpose
-------
Reduce per-frame/background surface overhead in desktop/browser rendering by
loading the fully opaque pre-rendered ground with pygame Surface.convert()
instead of convert_alpha().

Changed
-------
- code/level.py
  - graphics/world/ground_lb.png now uses convert().
- tests/test_checkpoint_d2b_opaque_ground.py
  - guards that the ground remains 5120x3904 and fully opaque;
  - guards the specific convert() load path;
  - guards that the ground remains draw-only on LAYERS['ground'].
