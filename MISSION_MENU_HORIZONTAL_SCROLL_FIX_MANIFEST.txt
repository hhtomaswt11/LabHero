Mission menu horizontal-scroll containment

Observed QA bug
---------------
Mission screens could be dragged horizontally far beyond their visible green
menu panel, exposing a large grey area. pygame-menu enables horizontal and
vertical overflow by default.

Fix
---
Every pygame_menu.Menu created inside code/mission01.py through mission40.py now
uses:

    overflow=(False, True)

This disables horizontal overflow while preserving vertical scrolling. Mission
labels already rely on word wrapping for long prose, so the player no longer
needs to pan the whole mission canvas sideways.

Scope
-----
- 40 mission modules only.
- 202 mission menu/submenu constructors updated.
- Books, simulator, Settings, inventory and other menus are unchanged.
- No mission logic, validator, scientific value, save state, map, backend or
  deployment behaviour changed.

Regression guard
----------------
tests/test_mission_menu_horizontal_overflow.py verifies every mission menu keeps
horizontal overflow disabled.
