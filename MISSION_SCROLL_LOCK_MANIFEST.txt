Mission horizontal scroll lock

Problem
-------
In Web/Pygbag, pygame-menu 4.4.x could still translate the mission ScrollArea
horizontally from mouse/touch dragging even with horizontal overflow disabled.
That exposed the grey surface outside the fixed-width green mission panel.

Fix
---
The shared async menu runner now clamps horizontal scroll to the left edge on
every frame for menus whose title begins with "Mission ".

Vertical scrolling remains enabled.
Simulator, Books, Settings and other non-mission menus are unaffected.

No mission logic, scientific values, validators, save data, progression, map,
backend or deployment behavior changed.
