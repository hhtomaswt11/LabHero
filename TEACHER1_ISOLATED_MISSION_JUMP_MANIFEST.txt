TEACHER.1 — isolated mission jump

Web entry:
  ?teacher=1&mission=17

Desktop entry:
  python3 LabHero.py --teacher --mission 17

Behaviour:
- Bypasses title/student registration and opens the requested Mission 01-40 menu.
- Missions numerically before the target exist only inside the isolated teacher
  preview and are marked completed/activated there for coherent NPC progression.
- Physical progression gates are bypassed by CampaignContext('teacher').
- Press T in the world to reopen the requested mission menu.
- Student Final Results are disabled during teacher previews.
- Teacher writes use a separate persistence namespace and a fresh namespace is
  cleared when the preview starts. Normal student saves/localStorage are untouched.
- No mission thresholds, validation logic, scientific constants, map objects,
  backend API or deployment configuration were changed.
